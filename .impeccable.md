## Design Context

### Users
Golf Car drivers using the app during active work to review assigned requests, understand pickup and destination details quickly, and open coordinates for navigation.

### Brand Personality
Luxurious, refined, dependable. The interface should feel calm and premium while remaining immediately useful during operations.

### Aesthetic Direction
A dark, warm, refined visual system built around the existing espresso brown, ivory, and muted gold palette. Favor restrained hierarchy, precise spacing, elegant typography, and purposeful controls. Avoid decorative clutter, generic dashboard cards, loud gradients, and visual effects that compete with trip information.

### Design Principles
- Make the next driver action obvious without repeating information.
- Use typography, spacing, and subtle contrast instead of ornamental color strips.
- Keep navigation and time information reachable at a glance.
- Preserve strong Arabic and English readability and layout behavior.
- Every visual element must have an operational purpose.
